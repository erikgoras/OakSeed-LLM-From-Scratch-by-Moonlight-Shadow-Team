First principles mean starting from what is fundamentally true and building upward.
Simple, modular components are easier to inspect, test, and reason about over time.
A character tokenizer maps every symbol to a clear integer index and back again.
Attention lets each token weigh context from earlier positions in the sequence.
Small experiments validate assumptions quickly before complexity is allowed to grow.
Clear contracts between modules keep systems steady when details change.
Every iteration teaches something practical about architecture, data, and trade-offs.
Patience and clean structure beat clever shortcuts when building from scratch.
